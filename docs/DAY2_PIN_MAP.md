# Day 2 Pin Map

## Analogue inputs
A0 — Heart rate signal
A1 — SpO2 signal
A2 — Temperature signal
A3 — Respiratory rate signal
A4 — Systolic blood pressure signal
A5 — Diastolic blood pressure signal

## TFT
53 — CS
49 — D/C
48 — RESET
51 — MOSI
52 — SCK
5V — VCC
GND — GND

The six potentiometers emulate already-conditioned sensor outputs. They do not represent the sensing physics of medical instruments.
